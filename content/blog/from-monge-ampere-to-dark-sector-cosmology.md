My earlier research focused on cosmological structure formation in Monge–Ampère gravity. That programme led me through the weak-field Poisson limit, spherical collapse, perturbation theory, one-loop clustering, and halo statistics.

My current work on matter diffusion and interacting dark-sector models asks a different physical question, but several methodological themes remain: how a modification enters the cosmological equations, how it changes observables, and how theory should be confronted with data without hiding model assumptions.

The common thread is structure: the structure of the equations, the structure of the cosmic web, and the structure of an inference pipeline that can be tested and reproduced.
