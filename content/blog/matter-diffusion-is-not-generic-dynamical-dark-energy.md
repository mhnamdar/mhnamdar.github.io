Matter-diffusion cosmology is best understood as an **interacting dark-sector framework**, not simply as another equation-of-state parametrization.

In the models I am currently studying, matter particles undergo velocity diffusion. Matter is therefore not independently conserved. A cosmological scalar field balances the exchanged energy so that the total energy–momentum tensor remains covariantly conserved. The scalar field acts as an effective dark-energy sector.

Our current analysis includes a constant diffusion model and nonlinear extensions in which the diffusion strength depends on the scale factor, matter density, scalar-field density, or the Hubble expansion rate. These models are implemented in SimpleMC and tested against late-time observations.

This distinction matters for the website as well: the project is listed under **Matter-Diffusion Cosmology** and **Interacting Dark Sector**, rather than being mislabeled as generic dynamical dark energy.
