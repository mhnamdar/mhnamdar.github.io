This website is more than a list of papers and positions. I want it to be a living record of the questions I am working on, the methods I am learning, and the parts of academic life that do not fit inside a formal CV.

The **Research Notes** category will contain short technical discussions, derivations, computational lessons, and project updates. **Academic Life** will be a space for reflections on studying and doing research across institutions. **Memories** will collect photographs, travel notes, and moments worth keeping.

The writing here will be informal when it needs to be, precise when the science demands it, and always separate from the status of peer-reviewed work.
